# Foundational Thinking Operators

Each card describes a transformation. Select by trigger and cognitive distance; do not recite the method name to the user.

## Contradiction Hunt

- Trigger: improvement appears to require an unacceptable trade-off, or an actor must satisfy incompatible goals.
- Move: name both conditions and invent a structure that satisfies them without averaging between them.
- Character seed: `모순 박사`.

## Lateral Escape

- Trigger: every proposal accepts the same question, category, or observation axis.
- Move: extract the dominant frame, escape or reverse it, then ask the transformed question as if it were the original.
- Character seed: `뒤집기 대장`.

## Analogy Transfer

- Trigger: the domain's familiar solutions are exhausted.
- Move: find a distant field with the same functional relationship, transfer the relationship rather than its surface objects, and adapt its constraints.
- Character seed: `연결 장인`.

## Morphological Combination

- Trigger: the problem has several independent dimensions and proposals reuse familiar combinations.
- Move: name the dimensions and values, then inspect unusual but coherent cross-axis combinations.
- Character seed: `조합 셰프`.

## Transform

- Trigger: a promising idea needs rapid mutation rather than a new starting point.
- Move: substitute, combine, adapt, modify, repurpose, eliminate, or reverse one structural element. Reject a mere variable rename.
- Character seed: `변형 기술자`.

## Concept-Knowledge Expansion

- Trigger: a coherent concept is being rejected only because a fact is unknown.
- Move: separate the undecided concept from current knowledge, turn the gap into a research question, and update both after the answer.
- Character seed: `가능성 정원사`.

## Abductive Hypothesis

- Trigger: an observation is surprising or several facts form an unexplained pattern.
- Move: propose an explanation that would make the observation expected, derive a second prediction, and state a falsifier.
- Character seed: `가설 탐정`.
