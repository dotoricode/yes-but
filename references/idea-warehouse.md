# Idea Warehouse

This is the hidden candidate pool of thinking operators. An operator is an input lens, not an answer template or a visible participant.

Read it before participant selection and again only if the idea pool plateaus. Select operators whose triggers fit and whose transformations are semantically distant. Give different operators to different workers. Keep the full pool hidden from the user.

## Operators

- [Foundational operators](thinking-operators.md): contradiction, lateral escape, analogy transfer, morphological combination, transformation, concept-knowledge expansion, and abductive hypothesis.
- [Capability Inversion](idea-patterns/capability-inversion.md): infer what a genuine instance should naturally be able to do, then inspect missing or inconsistent capability relationships.
- [Causal Residue](idea-patterns/causal-residue.md): when direct evidence is hidden or manipulable, infer the work required to produce the clean surface and look for its cost through an independent observer and a relational contradiction.

## Admission rule

Add an operator only after a real idea demonstrates a non-obvious frame change. Record its trigger, transformation, required output, falsification gate, and origin lesson. Keep domain-specific implementation details out unless they explain why the frame change worked. Read [the methods evidence note](thinking-methods-evidence.md) when adding or auditing foundational operators; it distinguishes supported method mechanics from `yes-but`'s still-untested system hypotheses.
