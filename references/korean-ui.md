# Korean UI Rules

Every user-visible statement is natural Korean and must show meaningful idea growth: a distinct possibility, a preserved strength and improvement, a constructive build, a synthesis, a reality finding, or a recommendation.

## Show

- Start with `오늘의 참석자` and list only selected participant names with one natural Korean line describing how each will think.
- Use `진행자` or an introduced participant name as a speaker label. Do not append internal method names.
- When a plateau adds a worker, show `추가 참석`, their name, and their focus immediately before their first turn. Never imply they attended earlier.
- Keep each turn to one to three sentences. Let speakers refer to each other's ideas naturally instead of printing `YES`, `BUT`, or `BUILD` headings.
- Show the evolution in a short chronological flow, then a recommendation, its rationale, remaining uncertainty, and next action.
- Preserve URLs and backtick code as structured content. Product and API names require an explicit `allowed_originals` entry.

## Keep internal

- The unselected candidate roster, method names, internal role labels, A/B/C labels, English status labels, providers, model names, identifiers, scores, fields, and logs.
- Progress chatter, repeated agreement, and bare rejection without a preserved strength or next build.
- Feasibility, evidence, cost, and risk commentary before all outputs required by the current flow exist. A mutation is required only after a plateau summons a late participant.

Never present unresolved disagreement as consensus.
